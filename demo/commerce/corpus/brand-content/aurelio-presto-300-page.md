# Aurelio Presto 300 — small footprint, full crema

Brand content supplied by Aurelio (a fictional brand of the Fictive Retail
bundle). Page as published 4 May 2026.

The Presto 300 is the newest machine in the Presto line, built for tight
counters: a slimmer chassis than any Presto before it, a quieter pump, and
the new auto-dose tray that meters ground coffee without a tamper. The
tray accepts Aurelio's own hopper or a compatible third-party grinder.

The Presto 300 pairs with the Aurelio Home app over the BrewLink Hub for
shot timing and descale reminders. Register your machine with Aurelio to
extend the warranty to three years.
