# Northglade G4: using the G4 with auto-dose espresso machines

Brand content supplied by Northglade (a fictional brand of the Fictive
Retail bundle). Guide as published 2 April 2026.

The Northglade G4 burr grinder can feed an auto-dose espresso machine such
as the Aurelio Presto 300 directly. Fitting requires two things: the dose
adapter ring DR-2, which seats the G4's spout on the machine's tray collar,
and grinder firmware 2.1, which adds the dose-complete signal the tray
waits for.

Check your firmware version in the G4's settings menu and update over USB
with the Northglade desktop tool. Without the DR-2 ring the spout does not
seal, and without firmware 2.1 the tray times out and discards the dose.
