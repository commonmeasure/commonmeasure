# Aurelio BrewLink Hub — your Presto, connected

Brand content supplied by Aurelio (a fictional brand of the Fictive Retail
bundle). Page as published 10 February 2026.

The BrewLink Hub is how a Presto talks to the Aurelio Home app: plug the
Hub into your router, pair it with your machine, and the app tracks shot
timing and descale reminders wherever you are.

The Hub is required for app features on every Presto machine; without it
the app cannot see your machine. One Hub serves every Aurelio machine in
the home.
