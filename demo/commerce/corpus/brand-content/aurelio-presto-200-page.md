# Aurelio Presto 200 — the heart of the home bar

Brand content supplied by Aurelio (a fictional brand of the Fictive Retail
bundle). Page as published 12 November 2025.

The Presto 200 is Aurelio's flagship espresso machine: a 15-bar pump, a
warming rack for two cups, and the dose dial our baristas tune by hand.
Available in three finishes — slate, oat and copper — the Presto 200 brings
café texture to the home counter.

Pair it with the Aurelio milk wand accessory for microfoam, and register
your machine with Aurelio to extend the warranty to three years. The Presto
200 carries the classic tamper-first workflow; it has no auto-dose tray and
we think purists prefer it that way.
