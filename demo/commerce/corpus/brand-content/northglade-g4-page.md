# Northglade G4 burr grinder — forty settings, zero fuss

Brand content supplied by Northglade (a fictional brand of the Fictive
Retail bundle). Page as published 30 March 2026.

The Northglade G4 is our flagship burr grinder: forty grind settings, a
low-retention chute, and burrs you can swap without tools. RRP £159.00.

To feed an auto-dose espresso machine such as the Aurelio Presto 300, fit
the dose adapter ring DR-2 and update to grinder firmware 2.1 over USB
with the Northglade desktop tool; without both, the machine's tray cannot
take a dose from the G4. For manual workflows the G4 works out of the box.
