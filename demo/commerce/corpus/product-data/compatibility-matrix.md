# Fictive Retail compatibility matrix: grinders and espresso machines

Structured product data from the Fictive Retail merchandising system.
Record updated: 1 August 2026.

| Grinder | Machine | Compatible | Requires |
|---|---|---|---|
| Northglade G4 | Aurelio Presto 300 | yes | dose adapter ring DR-2, grinder firmware 2.1 |
| Northglade G4 | Aurelio Presto 200 | no | — |

From 28 March 2026, the Northglade G4 burr grinder is compatible with the
Aurelio Presto 300 auto-dose tray when fitted with the dose adapter ring
DR-2 and running grinder firmware 2.1 or later. Earlier G4 firmware does not
signal dose completion to the tray, and no adapter is made for the
discontinued Presto 200.
