# Customer communications guidance

When communicating about the energy price cap:

- Say "the cap limits unit rates and standing charges", never "the cap caps
  your bill".
- Name the cap period explicitly (for example "1 July to 30 September"),
  because the figure changes quarterly and undated statements go stale.
- Link the current Ofgem announcement rather than restating its figure, so
  the primary evidence travels with the claim.
- Do not compare the cap to fixed-tariff prices without saying which cap
  period the comparison uses.
