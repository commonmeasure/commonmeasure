# Price cap briefing

Internal briefing note for teams answering questions about the Ofgem energy
price cap.

The price cap limits what suppliers may charge per unit of energy and per day
in standing charges for customers on default (standard variable) tariffs. It
does not cap a household's total bill: a household that uses more energy pays
more.

Ofgem reviews the cap quarterly. The widely reported headline figure is the
annualised cost for a household with typical consumption, which is an
illustration rather than a guarantee.

The current headline figure is **not** kept in this corpus. Always take it
from Ofgem's own quarterly announcement, which is the primary source.
